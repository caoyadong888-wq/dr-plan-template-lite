# Disaster Recovery Plan Template (lite)

## English

### Purpose

A free preview of the outline: the English plan and runbook templates, both contact-roster templates, both RTO/RPO worksheets, and the filled Example Labs roster and worksheet. The filled plan, the filled runbook, and the checker are not in this preview.

Durations in the filled worksheet are Example Labs' own choices for lab-wiki. They are not a vendor SLA. Mailboxes stay on example.com. The sample lead mailbox is `incident-lead@example.com`.

### Deliverables

- `deliverables/DR_PLAN.template.md` — English plan template with tokens to replace.
- `deliverables/RUNBOOK.template.md` — English runbook template.
- `deliverables/CONTACT_ROSTER.template.md` — English roster template.
- `deliverables/CONTACT_ROSTER.template.zh.md` — Chinese roster template.
- `deliverables/RTO_RPO_WORKSHEET.md` — English worksheet.
- `deliverables/RTO_RPO_WORKSHEET.zh.md` — Chinese worksheet.
- `examples/lab-wiki/CONTACTS.md` — filled English roster.
- `examples/lab-wiki/CONTACTS.zh.md` — filled Chinese roster.
- `examples/lab-wiki/rto_rpo.md` — filled English worksheet, RTO 4h and RPO 15m.
- `examples/lab-wiki/rto_rpo.zh.md` — filled Chinese worksheet.

### Quick start

Read `deliverables/RTO_RPO_WORKSHEET.md`, then the filled sheet `examples/lab-wiki/rto_rpo.md`. Replace tokens in the plan template only after the team has chosen its own durations.
